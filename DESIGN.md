# Paclé visual system

## Direction

A dark, spacious studio surface anchored in the supplied Figma composition: huge white/lavender typography and a luminous, masked motion field. It is intentionally restrained: light comes from the particle field and visual artwork, not from extra gradients, outlines, or decorative UI.

## Tokens

- Ground: `#0a0910`
- Ink: `#f7f5ff`
- Lavender: `#c2b8ff`
- Deep violet: `#6b0081`
- Muted ink: `#ada9b8`
- Display: Inter, fallback system sans

## Surface rules

- Glass is translucent with blur and soft shadow; it has no visible border.
- The only outlined element is the final Contact button, matching the supplied footer reference.
- Cards use 16px corners and a deep, diffuse shadow; avoid nested cards.
- Typography supplies the emphasis: lavender, italic, semibold, and tightly tracked display type.
- Motion is quiet and purposeful: the particle field and one-time reveal sequence; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
