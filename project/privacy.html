Terms of Use

Welcome to Paclé. We’re a team of four IT students building digital projects and looking for real-world partnerships.

These terms cover our public website, including our portfolio, project previews, links, and contact form. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean the Paclé team.

By using this website, you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please stop using it.

1. About Paclé

This is our team portfolio. It introduces our members, skills, work, and community affiliations. You can use it to learn about us or start a conversation about a thesis, capstone partnership, website, or other digital project.

2. Eligibility and Use of the Website

You don’t need an account to browse. Please use the site lawfully and respectfully. Don’t send spam, upload harmful content, interfere with the site, or try to access systems without permission.

If you contact us for a school, business, or organization, make sure you have permission to represent them and share their information.

3. Website Forms and Submissions

Our contact form opens a draft in your email app. Review it and press send there to deliver your inquiry. Clicking “Send inquiry” on the site alone does not send an email.

Give us accurate details and a short description of your idea. Please leave out passwords, government IDs, payment details, and private records. If the form doesn’t open your email app, use the address in Contact us.

4. Projects and Partnerships

An inquiry starts a conversation. It does not book our team or create a project agreement. We’ll discuss the scope, schedule, responsibilities, and any costs before agreeing to work together.

Portfolio examples show our experience. They aren’t a promise that every project will have the same features, timeline, or results.

5. Affiliations

Community and organization names describe our members’ affiliations. Their appearance does not mean those organizations sponsor Paclé or endorse every project. Their names and logos belong to their respective owners.

6. No Guarantee of Accuracy or Availability

We try to keep the site accurate, but project details, links, and availability can change. We can’t promise that everything will always be current or error-free. Please confirm important details with us before making plans.

7. Intellectual Property

Please ask before reusing our branding or project materials, and don’t present them as your own. Materials with a separate license follow that license.

8. User-Submitted Information

Your ideas and materials remain yours. Sending them lets us review them and discuss your inquiry with you. It does not give us permission to publish your private materials or claim ownership of them.

Only share files and information you have permission to send. If your project needs a confidentiality agreement, discuss that with us before sharing sensitive details.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

Privacy Policy

You can browse our portfolio without creating an account or sending us personal details.

This policy explains what happens when you visit the site or contact our four-person team. It covers this website and our inquiry emails, not separate websites linked from our portfolio.

Information we receive

When you send an email, we receive your sender address, name, message, and anything else you include, such as your organization or project details.

The contact form asks for your name, email, organization, and message. It currently puts your name and message into an email draft. The email and organization fields are not automatically copied into that draft. We receive the email only after you send it from your email app.

The site does not save form entries to a website database. Hosting and external resource providers may process technical details such as your IP address, browser, and request time when delivering the site.

How we use your information

We use inquiry emails to reply, understand your project, and arrange follow-up conversations. If we work together, we may keep relevant messages to manage that project. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Cookies and external services

The current site does not include advertising trackers or an analytics tool. Our own code does not set tracking cookies or store form entries in your browser.

We load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs. Your browser contacts those providers to display the page, which can share your IP address and browser details with them. Your email provider also handles messages you choose to send. These services follow their own privacy policies.

Who can access your information

Team members involved in your inquiry may need to read your message. Email and hosting providers process information needed to run their services and may do so in other countries. We may also need to disclose information when legally required.

We don’t sell inquiry information or use it for advertising. We won’t publish your private project materials without your permission.

Storage and security

Inquiry emails remain in the email services used for our correspondence. We keep relevant messages while handling an inquiry or project, or when needed for records or legal obligations. There is no automatic deletion period built into this website.

No website or email service can guarantee complete security. Please share only what we need to understand your idea, and leave sensitive records out of your first message.

Your choices

You can choose not to contact us, or close the email draft without sending it. You can also ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or ask us to stop using it. Email us with your request; we may need to confirm it is yours before acting.

Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations. Where Philippine privacy law applies, you can also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission.

Have a question about this page or want to ask about your information? Email the Paclé team. For privacy requests, “Privacy request” is a helpful subject line.
